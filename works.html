Works

Detailed explorations of individual pieces

Untitled I

Oil on canvas 24 × 30 in 2023

Description

This painting began as an exploration of warm tones and their emotional resonance. The layering of colors creates depth and movement, inviting the viewer to find their own narrative. The surface is built up over time, with each layer responding to the previous one.

The work is available for viewing and discussion. Please inquire for availability and pricing.

Night Field

Acrylic on canvas 30 × 40 in 2023

Description

Inspired by nocturnal landscapes and the feeling of quiet solitude. This piece uses darker tones and gestural marks to evoke atmosphere and introspection. The composition is intentionally asymmetrical, creating tension and visual interest.

Soft Drift

Mixed media 28 × 36 in 2024

Description

A meditation on movement and impermanence. This mixed media work combines paint, graphite, and collage to create a sense of flow and transition. The title reflects the gentle quality of the composition.
