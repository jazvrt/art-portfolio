About

I create paintings that explore texture, color, and emotional resonance. My work is driven by intuition and the desire to communicate without words. Each piece is an attempt to capture a moment, a feeling, or a memory translated into visual form.

Alongside my visual practice, I write poetry as a parallel reflection on the themes present in my work.
