Artist statement

Making from instinct, rhythm, and memory.

I build work through gesture, texture, and restraint. My paintings begin with feeling and then become structure.

I'm interested in what remains after the first impulse fades — what is left in the material, in the silence, in the marks that keep their own logic.

Writing

Poetry

In the space between thought and color,
where language fails,
the image arrives.

Shadow is not absence.
It is a way of listening.
I paint what the silence keeps.

What is made by hand
is never only form.
It is a weather of feeling.
