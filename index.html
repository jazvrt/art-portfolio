Artist statement

Painting from instinct, rhythm, and memory.

I create work that sits between abstraction and atmosphere. My paintings explore movement, gesture, and emotional residue—using texture, line, and a restrained palette to let feeling lead the composition.

Each piece begins with a feeling or a memory, then evolves through layered marks and shifting tones. The goal is not to explain, but to leave space for the viewer to feel their own response.
