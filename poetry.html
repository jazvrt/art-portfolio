Poetry

Words as a mirror to the visual practice

Untitled Reflection I

In the space between thought and color,
where language fails,
I find the image.

Each mark is a breath,
each layer a conversation
with what came before.

The canvas holds what words cannot.

Night Study

Darkness is not absence.
It is possibility.

In the quiet hours,
forms emerge that daylight erases.
Shadows have their own language.

I listen. I paint what I hear.

On Making

There is no perfection,
only intention.

The hand moves without plan.
The brush knows what the mind cannot explain.

In this not-knowing,
something true arrives.

Soft Edges

Everything bleeds into everything else.
Boundaries dissolve.

What was solid becomes breathing.
What was separate becomes whole.

This is where I live—
in the soft edges of becoming.
