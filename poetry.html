Writing

Poetry

The full collection, in order.

01

In the space between thought and color,
where language fails,
the image arrives.

02

Shadow is not absence.
It is a way of listening.
I paint what the silence keeps.

03

What is made by hand
is never only form.
It is a weather of feeling.

04

Some mornings the canvas
already knows.
I only follow the hand that remembers.

05

There is a color for waiting.
I have mixed it many times
and never named it right.

06

The line does not lie.
It only says what the hand
was too slow to hide.

07

A room becomes quiet
the way paint becomes still —
slowly, then all at once.

08

I keep the failed paintings.
They taught the good ones
how to fall correctly.

09

Memory arrives unevenly,
like light through a curtain —
I paint the gaps, not the sun.

10

Stillness is not empty.
It is the whole day
held in one held breath.
